Privacy Policy for Meeting Nudge

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Meeting Nudge ("the app") is developed by Chirag Garg. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

Meeting Nudge requests the following permissions on your device:

  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR) — used to read your event titles, times, and mute preferences so the app can schedule alarms for them.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — used to show alarm notifications.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM) — used to fire alarms at the precise start time of your events.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — used to re-schedule your alarms after your device restarts.
  • Vibrate / Wake Lock — used to vibrate and wake your screen when an alarm fires.

How your data is used

All calendar data read by Meeting Nudge stays on your device. The app does not transmit, upload, sell, or share your calendar events, event titles, or any other personal data with the developer or any third party. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in this app. Your event data is used solely to schedule local alarms and display them within the app.

Data storage

Your alarm preferences (lead time, sync schedule, per-event mute settings) are stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore. This data is not backed up to any server the developer controls.

Data sharing

Meeting Nudge does not share any data with third parties. The app has no network permissions and does not communicate with any external server.

Children's privacy

Meeting Nudge is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date.
